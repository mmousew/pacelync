Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PaceLync LLC handles information when you visit this website or contact us. This website is intended to provide information about our company and does not require you to create an account.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information included in your message, such as your name, email address, and any details you choose to provide. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and maintain relevant business records.

Website data

This website does not use advertising cookies or sell personal information. Our hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and timestamps, to deliver the website, maintain security, and diagnose technical issues.

How we use information

  • To respond to business, product, and support inquiries.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate our website and communications, or when required by law. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information you have provided, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

Cookies and similar technologies

PaceLync LLC does not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies on this company website. Essential technical storage may be used to remember preferences, such as your selected language, and to provide core website functionality.

Information security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International visitors

If you access this website from outside the location where our hosting providers operate, information may be processed in another country. We take reasonable steps to work with providers that maintain appropriate safeguards.

Children

This company website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

Changes

We may update this policy as our website or practices change. The date above identifies the latest version.
